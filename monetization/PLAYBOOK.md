# Monetization playbook (YouTube Shorts)

Plain map of **paths to revenue** from Shorts. This is strategy, not legal advice. **Check current YouTube Partner Program (YPP) rules, ad policies, and disclosure requirements** before you rely on any path below—YouTube changes eligibility, geography, and formats over time.

---

## Paths overview

| Path | What it is | Shorts role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **YPP / ads on Shorts** | Monetization through the YouTube Partner Program when you qualify. | Qualified views and policy-safe content; retention helps. |
| **Shorts revenue sharing / funds** | Program details and availability vary—**verify in YouTube Help & Studio**, not in this doc. | Same: eligible account + compliant content. |
| **Affiliate** | You earn when someone buys through your tracked link. | Demo, comparison, “what I’d use” Shorts; **disclose** on-screen and in description. |
| **Your offer** | Course, template, service, community, etc. | Short teases one outcome; CTA to landing page or pinned comment. |
| **Lead gen** | Email list, waitlist, free resource. | Short delivers one tip; CTA to opt-in. |
| **Bridge to long-form** | Full videos on the same channel or site. | Short is the hook; CTA to “full breakdown” video or playlist. |
| **Brand deals / sponsors** | Paid integration when you have fit and reach. | Dedicated Short or integrated mention; contract + disclosure. |

**Not listed:** fake income claims, “guaranteed” RPM numbers, or credential cosplay. Log real numbers only in `analytics/`.

---

## Eligibility & compliance (always verify)

- **YPP:** Subscriber, watch time, and policy requirements change—confirm in [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/) and **YouTube Studio → Earn**.
- **Ad-friendly content:** Controversial, repetitive, or misleading topics can limit ads—see **Advertiser-friendly content guidelines**.
- **Disclosures:** Paid promotion, affiliate links, and free product require clear disclosure (on-screen + description where applicable).
- **Shorts vs long-form:** Some monetization features attach to the **channel**, but viewer behavior differs by format—track Shorts separately in analytics.

## Decision tree: pillar → monetization path

```text
START: Which pillar is this Short?

├─ Monetization literacy
│   ├─ Goal: qualify for / keep YPP → Path: ads (if eligible) + subscribe + policy-safe topics
│   └─ Goal: teach trust → Path: lead gen (newsletter) or bridge to long-form explainer
│
├─ Offer architecture
│   ├─ Physical or SaaS tool you use → Path: affiliate (disclosed) OR your bundle
│   ├─ Your method / templates → Path: digital product or lead magnet
│   └─ High-touch service → Path: lead gen → sales call / application
│
├─ Retention mechanics
│   ├─ Pure education, no pitch → Path: ads (if eligible) + subscribe for series
│   └─ Series with payoff → Path: bridge to long-form or product drop in finale
│
├─ Batch & systems
│   ├─ Repo / template you sell → Path: digital product
│   └─ Free value only → Path: subscribe + lead gen for “batch planner” download
│
└─ Proof without flex
    ├─ Tool you actually use → Path: affiliate (disclosed) or none
    ├─ Process you productized → Path: digital product
    └─ Credibility only → Path: lead gen or consulting CTA
```

When two paths fit, pick **one primary CTA** per Short (see `monetization/OFFER-MAP.md`).

## Funnel shapes (simple)

**Shorts-only nurture:** Short → subscribe → more Shorts → offer in pinned comment / link in bio when ready.

**Short → long:** Short teases one question → “full answer” video → end-screen offer or affiliate in description.

**Short → owned list:** Short → free PDF / checklist → email sequence → paid offer.

Use `calendar/30-DAY-PLAN.md` to rotate pillars so you are not pitching every day.
