## Batch day checklist (repeat)

- [ ] Pick 3–5 ideas from `BACKLOG-SEED.md` same pillar or mixed
- [ ] Hooks on teleprompter or sticky notes from `STARTER-HOOKS.md`
- [ ] One wardrobe, one lighting setup, multiple tops optional
- [ ] Export vertical 9:16; captions burned or SRT
- [ ] Copy publish packs; schedule in Studio

---

## Review day checklist (repeat)

- [ ] Paste Studio metrics into `analytics/METRICS-TEMPLATE.md`—no invented numbers
- [ ] One hook to retire, one to repeat
- [ ] Update `monetization/OFFER-MAP.md` tracking column with what you actually used
- [ ] Move next ideas from backlog → `IDEA-TEMPLATE.md` files when approved
