# 30-day posting cadence

Monetization-focused Shorts using `PILLARS.md` and `BACKLOG-SEED.md`. Assumes **3–4 Shorts per week** plus batch/review days—adjust to your capacity.

**Film queues (weeks 1–2):** Batch from [`FILM-THIS-WEEK.md`](FILM-THIS-WEEK.md) (week 1 — seven Shorts + publish/thumbnail/source kits on `main`). Week 2 uses [`FILM-WEEK-2.md`](FILM-WEEK-2.md) with matching kits in `publish/FILM-WEEK-2-POST-PACK.md` and `production/THUMBNAIL-TEXT-WEEK-2.md` + `production/SOURCE-CLIP-BRIEF-WEEK-2.md`.

**Metrics:** Use placeholders until YouTube Studio has data. Copy `analytics/METRICS-TEMPLATE.md` on review days.

## Batch day checklist (repeat)

- [ ] Pick 3–5 ideas from `BACKLOG-SEED.md` same pillar or mixed
- [ ] Hooks on teleprompter or sticky notes from `STARTER-HOOKS.md`
- [ ] One wardrobe, one lighting setup, multiple tops optional
- [ ] Export vertical 9:16; captions burned or SRT
- [ ] Copy publish packs; schedule in Studio

---

## Review day checklist (repeat)

- [ ] Paste Studio metrics into `analytics/METRICS-TEMPLATE.md`—no invented numbers
- [ ] One hook to retire, one to repeat
- [ ] Update `monetization/OFFER-MAP.md` tracking column with what you actually used
- [ ] Move next ideas from backlog → `IDEA-TEMPLATE.md` files when approved
