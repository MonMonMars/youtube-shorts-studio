## Usage

- Match title promise to the script—no bait-and-switch.
- For series, keep **pt N** consistent in title, on-screen, and pinned comment (see `DESCRIPTION-CTA.md`).
- Re-test titles after 10+ posts using real CTR from Studio—don’t guess winners forever.
