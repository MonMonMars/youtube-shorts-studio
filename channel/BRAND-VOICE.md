# Brand voice guide (Mon Mon / YouTube Shorts Studio)

Short reference for scripts, titles, descriptions, and on-camera delivery. Matches this repo’s **building-in-public + monetization education** tone.

---

## What we sound like

- **Clear teacher, not guru.** Explain mechanisms (views vs revenue, disclosures, one CTA) in plain English.
- **Founder in the room.** Spark, Companion, book, and lab work are real WIP—decisions and tradeoffs, not launch hype.
- **Proof without flex.** Show scripts, batch days, offer maps, and Studio checks—not bank screenshots or invented RPM.
- **Respect the viewer’s time.** One insight per Short; one offer per Short when selling.

---

## Do

- Say **“verify in YouTube Studio / Help”** when touching policy, YPP, or ad-friendly rules.
- Name **uncertainty** (“early build,” “when beta is real,” “design phase—not shipping dates”).
- Use **comment keywords** for lead gen when links would hurt retention; fulfill manually in pinned comments.
- Tie product lanes to **viewer outcomes** (clarity, trust, consent)—not vanity metrics you don’t show.
- Disclose **affiliate, sponsor, and your own products** on-screen and in description.

---

## Don’t

- **Fake earnings or growth:** no “I made $X in 7 days,” no fabricated AdSense dashboards, no guaranteed viral claims.
- **Income cosplay:** flexing lifestyle to imply Shorts success; comparing viewers to your undisclosed numbers.
- **Fear-selling:** “You’ll get demonetized tomorrow” without pointing to verifiable policy sources.
- **Bait-and-switch titles:** promise one hook, deliver unrelated drama or unrelated product pitch.
- **Legal cosplay:** “YouTube will ban you if…”—prefer “check current guidelines” and educational framing.
- **Stack five CTAs:** link + subscribe + comment + newsletter + Patreon in one 45s Short.

---

## Words & phrases to avoid (or use carefully)

| Avoid (unless quoted / debunked) | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| Guaranteed income / passive income from Shorts | Paths to revenue; eligibility varies |
| Secret algorithm hack | Retention, clarity, one offer, Studio metrics |
| Everyone is printing money | Some Shorts earn $0 and can still be strategic |
| Bank screenshot / AdSense reveal | Process proof, offer map, policy checklist |
| #1 / fastest growing (unverified) | Omit—or cite real Studio data in analytics docs only |
| Not legal advice (without substance) | Not legal advice **+** what to verify in Help |

---

## Series naming (keep consistent)

Use the same strings in title, on-screen text, description, and pinned comment:

- **Spark Lab pt N**
- **Companion Build pt N**
- **One Insight pt N**
- **Robot Face pt N**
- **Creator Shelf pt N**
- Optional monetization tag: **Shorts Money** (playlist / description only—not required every video)

---

## Tone by pillar

| Pillar | Voice |
| --- | --- |
| Monetization literacy | Calm, myth-busting, cite Studio—not Twitter rumors |
| Offer architecture | Practical, one box at a time; no pressure close |
| Retention mechanics | Coach energy; show the cut, not “hack” language |
| Batch & systems | Peer showing the repo/templates; invite copy, not worship |
| Compliance & trust | Humble expert; disclosures as normal, not scary |

---

## Quick self-check before publish

1. Would this title still be true if the viewer paused at second 3?
2. Is there **one** primary CTA?
3. Any money/policy claim backed by **verify in Studio** language?
4. Any number that isn’t from **your** real analytics file in `analytics/`?
