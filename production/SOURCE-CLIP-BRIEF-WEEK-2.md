# Source clip brief — film week 2 (7 Shorts)

One paragraph per Short: what to **capture in camera roll or screen record** so Path A edit, Path B AI restyle ([`AI-PIPELINE.md`](AI-PIPELINE.md)), or Path C B-roll assembly has enough material. Targets assume **vertical 9:16**, eye-level face cam unless noted. For Higgsfield Shorts Studio, a usable **source** is often **~30–90s** of stable A-roll plus key inserts—not the final captioned master.

Scripts and shot IDs: [`calendar/FILM-WEEK-2.md`](../calendar/FILM-WEEK-2.md).

## 1. Hook that pays rent

**Target:** ~35–45s total capture; **30–50s** clean talking-head for optional AI restyle. **Framing:** chest-up face cam; **DK-07** timer start/stop at 0:00 with hard jump cut at ~0:02 to payoff visual (before/after edit, blurred metric, or satisfying cut). **Must be on screen:** hook text **+5 SEC**; beats **GAP → not lecture**, **SHOW PAYOFF EARLY**, **ONE BEAT ONLY** from [`THUMBNAIL-TEXT-WEEK-2.md`](THUMBNAIL-TEXT-WEEK-2.md). **YT-01** optional with all numbers blurred—no fake retention stats. Keep motion in first 3s; stable exposure for Shorts Studio upload.

## 2. Affiliate without cringe

**Target:** ~50–60s; **45–70s** mixed face + product hands. **Framing:** open with cringe-face beat then calm demo; **20s continuous “in use”** clip (mic, light, notebook—real workflow, not unboxing theater). **Must be on screen:** **YT-03** description paste showing `#ad` / affiliate disclosure example (no private data); **DISCLOSE ONCE** card or spoken line; say comment keyword **TOOL** clearly twice. **PH-01** wake optional; **DK-04** laptop close for punctuation optional. No fake “limited time” graphics.

## 3. Spark Lab pt 3 — monetization without ruining the match

**Target:** ~45–55s; **50–80s** if using Shorts Studio (sketch + UI helps). **Framing:** **DK-06** two-column or three-box sketch—“free user” vs “paid tier”—**no fake price tags**; **PH-02** blurred feed; **PH-03** optional Spark UI motion blur. **Must be on screen:** **SPARK LAB PT 3** hook; beats **PAY FOR DEPTH**, **ONE CLEAR UPGRADE**, **FRAMEWORK FIRST**; speak **PAYWALL** keyword for CTA. Face cam between sketch updates; keep labels in middle safe zone.

## 4. Companion Build pt 3 — iPhone-first, not web wrapper

**Target:** ~40–50s; **40–65s** POV-heavy source. **Framing:** **PH-04** over-shoulder phone in hand; cross out or cut away from desktop browser “wrapper” gag (paper X or monitor off-frame). **Must be on screen:** **COMPANION BUILD PT 3**; beats **POCKET PRESENCE**, **ONE PLATFORM WELL**, **SAY NO OUT LOUD**; list day-one nos verbally or on sticky notes (**DK-03**). **PH-03** optional WIP companion frame. No Android/iPad fake parity UI—honest scope only.
