# Source clip brief — film week 4 (7 Shorts)

One paragraph per Short: what to **capture in camera roll or screen record** so Path A edit, Path B AI restyle ([`AI-PIPELINE.md`](AI-PIPELINE.md)), or Path C B-roll assembly has enough material. Targets assume **vertical 9:16**, eye-level face cam unless noted. For Higgsfield Shorts Studio, a usable **source** is often **~30–90s** of stable A-roll plus key inserts—not the final captioned master.

Scripts and shot IDs: [`calendar/FILM-WEEK-4.md`](../calendar/FILM-WEEK-4.md).
