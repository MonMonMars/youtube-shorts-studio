# B-roll & screen-record shot bank

Reusable shots for **founder**, **app**, and **monetization literacy** Shorts. Film vertical 9:16; keep clips 2–5s for fast cuts. No fake metrics on screen—use blurred placeholders or your real Studio (redact).
