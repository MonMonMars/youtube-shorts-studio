# Source clip brief — film week (7 Shorts)

One paragraph per Short: what to **capture in camera roll or screen record** so Path A edit, Path B AI restyle ([`AI-PIPELINE.md`](AI-PIPELINE.md)), or Path C B-roll assembly has enough material. Targets assume **vertical 9:16**, eye-level face cam unless noted. For Higgsfield Shorts Studio, a usable **source** is often **~30–90s** of stable A-roll plus key inserts—not the final captioned master.

Scripts and shot IDs: [`calendar/FILM-THIS-WEEK.md`](../calendar/FILM-THIS-WEEK.md).

**Target:** ~45–60s total capture; **45–55s** clean talking-head for optional AI restyle. **Framing:** chest-up, face cam; insert **YT-01** (Studio analytics with revenue/views fields **blurred or placeholder**—no fake dollar amounts) and **DK-06** (whiteboard or paper: three boxes labeled hook → value → offer, empty or lightly sketched). **Must be on screen:** hook beat “views vs pay” (split or cut between blurred view metric and empty revenue field); on-screen concept **ATTENTION vs PATH** and **INTRO ≠ CHECKOUT** either spoken or added in edit from [`THUMBNAIL-TEXT.md`](THUMBNAIL-TEXT.md). **DK-02** enter-frame hook take optional. Keep exposure steady for Shorts Studio upload.

**Target:** ~40–50s; **35–50s** single-take face cam OK for AI restyle. **Framing:** face cam with room to gesture—mime “link in bio” eye-roll and swipe-away in hook (**DK-02** optional). **Must be on screen:** **YT-03** (description box with disclosure example, no real private data) and **DK-04** or **DK-06** for “one CTA” beat; speak comment-keyword template clearly **twice** if using keyword CTA from [`publish/FILM-WEEK-POST-PACK.md`](../publish/FILM-WEEK-POST-PACK.md). ## 3. Shorts ad policy plain English

**Target:** ~50–60s; **40–70s** source for restyle (policy Shorts benefit from readable text cards). **Framing:** face cam plus **screen record YT-02** (YouTube Help search for advertiser-friendly guidance—use current official pages, not third-party blogs). **Must be on screen:** opening **NOT LEGAL ADVICE** card or spoken disclaimer; three rule beats with simple text cards **1 AD-FRIENDLY**, **2 DISCLOSURES**, **3 RE-CHECK** per script; **YT-01** blurred Studio optional. Avoid controversial B-roll; keep background neutral. Screen record legible at mobile scale or plan caption overlay in edit.
