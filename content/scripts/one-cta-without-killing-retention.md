| **CTA type** | comment (keyword lead gen) |

---

## Hook (0–3s)

**Visual:** Creator miming “link in bio” eye-roll; swipe away gesture.

**Spoken / on-screen text:**

> “Link in bio” at second fifty-nine makes people swipe. Try this instead.

**On-screen:** **DON’T END COLD**

**Pattern interrupt or promise:** Names a common pain; offers a specific alternative.

---

## Body

### Beat 1

> **Mid-Short micro-CTA:** after your best tip, say “Stick around—I’ll tell you how to get the template.” That’s retention bait, not a sales pitch.

**On-screen:** **MID = STICK**

### Beat 2

> **End with one keyword:** “Comment **TEMPLATE** and I’ll reply with how to grab the script layout.” You fulfill in pinned comment—no spam, one link.

**On-screen:** **COMMENT: TEMPLATE**

### Beat 3

> One offer. One word. Same promise as the video. If the Short is about hooks, the keyword isn’t “BUY NOW.”

**On-screen:** **ONE WORD = ONE OFFER**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- Show phone with comment section blurred except keyword
- Jump cut; keep pacing tight

---

## CTA (last 3–5s)

**Spoken:**

> Comment **TEMPLATE** below—I’ll pin the reply. Follow for the next CTA test.

**On-screen:**

- **COMMENT: TEMPLATE**
- **FOLLOW**

## Production notes

- Caption keywords to emphasize: TEMPLATE, pinned comment, one offer
- Music / SFX: ping on keyword reveal
- Disclosure on screen (if needed): if template links to paid product later, update pinned comment with disclosure
