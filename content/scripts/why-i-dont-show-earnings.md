## Body

### Beat 1

> **Income screenshots** teach you my month—not your levers. RPM moves with niche, geography, seasonality. Without context, it’s flex or fear, not a system.

**On-screen:** **CONTEXT > SCREENSHOT**

### Beat 2

> **Process proof:** scripts, batch days, offer maps, policy rewrites—the stuff you can copy without my AdSense login. That’s what this channel is for.

**On-screen:** **PROCESS PROOF**

### Beat 3

> When I *do* talk money, it’s **paths**: ads when eligible, offers, affiliates—with disclosures. Real numbers only from **my** Studio when I choose to share, never invented.

**On-screen:** **PATHS, NOT FLEX**
