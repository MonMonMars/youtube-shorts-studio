## Hook (0–3s)

**Visual:** Split screen: high view count graphic (placeholder) vs empty revenue field (placeholder—no fake dollars).

**Spoken / on-screen text:**

> Your Short blew up—and you made nothing. That’s not always a bug.

**On-screen:** **VIEWS ≠ PAY**

**Pattern interrupt or promise:** Validates frustration; reframes without hype.

---

## Body

### Beat 1

> Views measure **attention**. Revenue measures **a path**: YouTube Partner Program ads if you’re eligible—check current YPP rules in Studio—affiliate, your product, or a longer video that actually converts.

**On-screen:** **ATTENTION vs PATH**

### Beat 2

> Shorts often **introduce** you. If there’s no offer and no eligible ad pool, zero dollars can still be a win—if you sent people somewhere on purpose.

**On-screen:** **INTRO ≠ CHECKOUT**

### Beat 3

> Fix: pick **one path** for the next Short. Example—tease the full breakdown on your channel, or one comment keyword for a free map. Not all three.

**On-screen:** **PICK ONE PATH**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- Point at sticky note with “ONE PATH”
- No invented RPM or earnings numbers
