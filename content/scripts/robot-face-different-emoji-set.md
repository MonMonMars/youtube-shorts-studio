## Body

### Beat 1

> Human chat emoji assume **soft skin, tiny screen, social context**. A Unitree-class or Tesla-class body is loud, heavy, and in shared space—expressions need **clarity at distance**, not cute overload.

**On-screen:** **DISTANCE READS**

### Beat 2

> We’re building **B2B expression kits**: fewer states, higher contrast, slower transitions—trust signals, not cartoon mood swings. Lab footage only—no fake warehouse deploys.

**On-screen:** **LAB FOOTAGE**

### Beat 3

> If the face lies about what the robot knows—confused eyes while the arm moves—you lose the pilot. Honest states beat “happy” defaults.

**On-screen:** **HONEST STATES**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- RB-01 pattern cycle; RB-05 single eye blink reveal optional
- Label on-screen **NOT A PRODUCT LAUNCH** if showing panel
- No competitor bashing—problem space only
