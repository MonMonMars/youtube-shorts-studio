## Hook (0–3s)

**Visual:** Cringe face → cut to calm demo setup.

**Spoken / on-screen text:**

> If you’re shy about affiliate links—film *this* instead of a sales pitch.

**On-screen:** **NO CRINGE AFFILIATE**

**Pattern interrupt or promise:** Names the shame; offers a format swap.

---

## Body

### Beat 1

> Twenty seconds: show the thing **in use**. Not unboxing theater—real workflow. Mic, light, template, whatever you actually touch.

**On-screen:** **20s IN USE**

### Beat 2

> One sentence why **you** keep it. Not “best on earth”—“this solved X for me.” If you wouldn’t recommend it to a friend, don’t affiliate it.

**On-screen:** **ONE HONEST LINE**

### Beat 3

> Disclosure once—on-screen and in the description. **#ad** or “affiliate link.” No fake countdown, no “only today” unless it’s literally true.

**On-screen:** **DISCLOSE ONCE**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- B-roll: hands using product; face cam for disclosure beat
- Blur brand if policy-sensitive; keep motion readable

---

## CTA (last 3–5s)

> Comment **TOOL** if you want my affiliate checklist—not the links, the script shape. Follow for more offer architecture without the slime.

- **COMMENT: TOOL**
