## Body

### Beat 1

> A smiling face while the AI is **confused** trains distrust faster than any bug report. Companion avatars have to **match state**: listening, thinking, unsure, done.

**On-screen:** **FACE = STATE**

### Beat 2

> iPhone-first means micro-expressions at arm’s length—not uncanny Hollywood skin. I’d rather look **stylized and honest** than realistic and wrong.

**On-screen:** **HONEST > REALISTIC**

### Beat 3

> Retention isn’t tricking people to stay—it’s **relief** when the face tells the truth. Same pillar as Shorts: don’t promise what you can’t deliver.

**On-screen:** **RELIEF > TRICK**
