## Body

### Beat 1

> Version one: too much choice on first open. Version two: cleaner—but the primary action felt like a slot machine. Version three: one clear path to **start a conversation**, not endless browsing theater.

**On-screen:** **V1 → V2 → V3**

### Beat 2

> As a designer-founder, I’m optimizing for **respect and clarity**, not “time in app” for its own sake. Every extra button is a tax on someone who’s already tired of dating apps.

**On-screen:** **CLARITY > TRICKS**

### Beat 3

> I’m not showing DAU charts—I’m showing **why this button moved**. That’s the Spark Lab deal.

**On-screen:** **DECISIONS > CHARTS**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- PH-03 Figma/Xcode blur; PH-05 delete/cut gesture for “what I removed”
- Jump cuts on same frame with on-screen V1/V2/V3 labels
