## Hook (0–3s)

**Visual:** Finger hold-up “stop” gesture; quick zoom.

**Spoken / on-screen text:**

> Before you hit record—three seconds. Ready?

**On-screen:** **3-SEC CHECK**

**Pattern interrupt or promise:** Ultra-short promise; viewer expects a checklist they can use today.

---

## Body

Single main beat: three gates.

### Beat 1

> Gate one: **Policy.** Will this Short get limited ads or strike-adjacent? If you’re not sure, check current YouTube advertiser-friendly guidelines—not my opinion, YouTube’s.

**On-screen:** **1 POLICY** (blur any controversial example text)

### Beat 2

> Gate two: **One CTA.** Not five. One thing you want—subscribe, comment a word, or one link. If you can’t say it in one breath, simplify.

**On-screen:** **2 ONE CTA**

### Beat 3

> Gate three: **Disclosure.** Affiliate, gift, or your own product? Say it on-screen and in the description before you publish.

**On-screen:** **3 DISCLOSE**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- B-roll: quick flash of script template + checklist emoji
- Jump cut between gates; keep energy up
