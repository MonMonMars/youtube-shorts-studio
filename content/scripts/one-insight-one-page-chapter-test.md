## Body

### Beat 1

> I’m writing a psychology book with **Book Writer** as an organizer—not a replacement for thinking. AI suggests structure; I still own the claim.

**On-screen:** **HUMAN OWNS THE CLAIM**

### Beat 2

> Stress test: say the insight to a friend who doesn’t read textbooks. If they ask “so what do I *do*?”—I add one concrete line, not three more citations.

**On-screen:** **SO WHAT? → ONE LINE**

### Beat 3

> Shorts are my **preview lane**. One idea, one page energy. No fake bestseller badges—just pages that earn their spot.

**On-screen:** **PREVIEW, NOT HYPE**

**On-screen text / B-roll notes:**

- B-roll: highlighted paragraph blur, outline doc, you closing a notebook
