# Product series lanes (founder Shorts)

Optional **series lanes** for Mon’s projects—building in public, honest process, no invented metrics. Each series maps to an existing pillar in [`PILLARS.md`](PILLARS.md). Rotate lanes so the channel stays about **Shorts systems** first; product Shorts are proof-of-work, not hard ads.

## Spark — dating app (monetization-focused)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Series name** | **Spark Lab** — shipping a dating app in public |
| **Audience** | Indie founders, product designers, and dev-curious viewers who want honest app-build logs—not “we hit #1 overnight” fiction. |
| **Primary pillar** | **Proof without flex** (process, tradeoffs, mistakes) |
| **Secondary pillar** | Offer architecture (waitlist / beta when real—not fake urgency) |

### Episodes (6)

| # | Title | Hook direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why I’m building a Tinder-like app *again* | “Everyone says dating apps are solved—here’s the gap I’m testing anyway.” |
| 2 | The one screen I redesigned three times | Hold up phone mock / Figma blur: “This button cost me a week.” |
| 3 | Monetization without ruining the match | “Free users need love too—here’s how I’m thinking about paywalls *before* code.” |
| 4 | What I cut from v1 to ship | Fast cuts of deleted features: “If it’s not core to the first date, it’s out.” |
| 5 | Building in public without fake traction | “I’m not showing download charts—I’m showing decisions.” |
| 6 | Beta invite: who it’s for (and who it’s not) | “If you hate swipe culture but still want to meet people—maybe this is for you.” |

**Soft CTA style:** “Follow for the next Spark Lab log.” Optional: “Link in bio when beta opens—no waitlist hype until it’s real.” Comment prompt: “What’s the one dating app feature you’d delete?”
