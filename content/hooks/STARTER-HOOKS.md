# Starter hooks (copy-ready)

Thirty openings grouped by pattern. Swap the bracketed words for your niche; keep the **structure**.

---

## Curiosity (6)

1. Nobody tells you this about [Shorts / your niche] until it’s too late.
2. The [tool / habit] that quietly changed my [results / workflow]—not what you think.
3. Why your [Shorts / posts] feel stuck after the first 2 seconds.
4. I stopped doing [common advice] and my [retention / leads] finally moved.
5. This one line in your script might be costing you [views / clicks].
6. What happens if you post [daily / weekly] without a [offer / CTA] plan?

---

## Mistake (6)

7. Stop saying “link in bio” if you want people to watch till the end.
8. You’re not shadowbanned—you’re breaking this one rule on camera.
9. I almost posted this Short. It would’ve trashed ad suitability—here’s the fix.
10. Three monetization mistakes beginners make in the first 10 seconds.
11. If your hook starts with “Hey guys,” rewind and delete it.
12. Don’t film the product pitch before you film the **proof**.

---

## List (6)

13. Three checks before you hit record on a money Short.
14. Five hooks you can film before lunch—steal these.
15. Four ways Shorts pay that aren’t “viral lottery.”
16. Two CTAs max. Actually—**one** is better. Here’s why.
17. Six words that kill retention (and what to say instead).
18. Three pillars every monetization Short channel needs.

---

## Challenge (6)

19. Film this hook in one take—no teleprompter.
20. Try this CTA for one week and track comments only.
21. Can you explain YPP eligibility in 15 seconds without guessing numbers? I’ll try.
22. Batch five hooks in 20 minutes—timer on screen.
23. Rewrite your worst-performing Short hook using this pattern.
24. Comment your niche—I’ll reply with one pillar fit from the map.

---

## Prove-it (6)

25. Watch the first 2 seconds—then I’ll show you the retention trick.
26. Same topic, two hooks—which one would you swipe on?
27. Here’s the before edit vs after edit on pacing—no fake stats.
28. I mapped one Short to one offer—screen recording in 30 seconds.
29. This checklist is in the repo—I'll show the exact file path.
30. Pinned comment test: I’ll reply to every **TEMPLATE** in 24 hours.

---
